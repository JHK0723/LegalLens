# Vision Document

## Project Name

LegalLens

## Tagline

AI-Powered Contract Analysis and Legal Assistant

## Target Users

LegalLens is designed for users who need a simple and efficient way to understand legal agreements without requiring advanced legal knowledge.

### Primary Users

• Individuals reviewing rental agreements, employment contracts, service agreements, and NDAs.

• Small business owners evaluating contracts before signing.

• Startup founders reviewing partnership and investment agreements.

### Secondary Users

• Law students studying legal contracts.

• Legal interns performing preliminary document reviews.

• Freelancers reviewing contracts from clients.

## Success Metrics

The success of LegalLens will be evaluated using the following measurable outcomes:

• Users successfully upload legal documents.

• AI generates clause summaries for every uploaded document.

• Risk and fairness scores are produced for each clause.

• Users can interact with the AI assistant to clarify contract terms.

• Previously analyzed contracts can be retrieved from the dashboard.

• Average document analysis time remains under one minute for standard contracts.

## Assumptions

• Users upload contracts in supported formats (PDF or DOCX).

• Users have internet connectivity while using the application.

• OpenAI services remain available during analysis.

• Contracts are primarily written in English.

• Users possess basic computer literacy.

## Constraints

• Only PDF and DOCX files are currently supported.

• Contract analysis depends on external AI APIs.

• Internet access is mandatory.

• Processing large contracts may take additional time.

• AI-generated analysis is intended to assist users and should not replace professional legal advice.
